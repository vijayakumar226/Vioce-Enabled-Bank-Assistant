SBI Customer Complaint and Grievance Channels

How to lodge a complaint with SBI:

1. Visit your nearest branch and request the complaint book.
   The branch will resolve complaints within 10 days.
   Complaint number sent by SMS immediately.

2. Call toll free:
   1800 425 3800 (24x7)
   1800 11 22 11 (24x7)
   080-26599990 (Toll number)

3. SMS: Send UNHAPPY to 8008202020

4. Email: contactcentre@sbi.co.in
          customercare@sbi.co.in

5. Online: www.sbi.co.in under Customer Care link
           onlinesbi.com Complaints link
           SBI YONO app Complaints link

Complaint resolution timeline:
- Branch resolves within 10 days
- Escalated to Local Head Office on Day 11
- Escalated to Corporate Centre on Day 16
- Maximum resolution time: 21 days (3 weeks)
- If not resolved in 30 days, approach Banking Ombudsman

For unauthorized electronic transactions:
Report immediately on 1800 11 11 09 (dedicated toll free)
Email: unauthorisedtransactions@sbi.co.in
SMS: Forward transaction SMS to 9223008333
