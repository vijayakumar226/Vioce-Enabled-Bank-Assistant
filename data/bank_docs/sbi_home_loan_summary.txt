SBI Home Loan Products and Key Details

Available home loan schemes:
1. SBI Home Loan Scheme
2. SBI Pre-approved Home Loan
3. SBI Yuva Home Loan
4. SBI Max Gain Home Loan
5. SBI Realty Home Loan
6. SBI NRI Home Loan
7. SBI Gram Niwas / Sahyog Niwas / Tribal Plus
8. SBI Green Home Loan

Purpose: Purchase, construction, extension,
repairs or renovation of residential house,
flat, plot of land, and furnishings.

Loan to Value Ratio (LTV):
- Up to Rs.20 Lakhs: maximum 90% of property value
- Rs.20 Lakhs to Rs.75 Lakhs: maximum 80%
- Above Rs.75 Lakhs: maximum 75%

Interest rate: Floating rate on daily reducing balance.
No fixed rate option currently available.
Penal interest: 2% per annum on irregular amount
if EMI unpaid for more than 30 days.

Loan tenure: Maximum 30 years or up to age 70,
whichever is earlier.

Pre-closure charges: NIL. No penalty for prepayment
on home loans regardless of source of funds.

Bounced cheque or ECS dishonour penalty: Rs.250/-

Security: Equitable or registered mortgage of the
property financed.

Insurance: Property must be insured for market value
covering fire, flood, and earthquake in joint names
of bank and borrower.

Processing fee: Rs.1000 + service tax (campaign rate).

Switching from fixed to floating rate:
Charge of 0.56% of outstanding loan amount.
