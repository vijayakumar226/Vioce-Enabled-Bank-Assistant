SBI Service Charges (Updated 30.09.2025)

Savings Bank Account:
- Cheque book: First 10 leaves free per year.
  10 leaf book Rs.40+GST, 25 leaf book Rs.75+GST
- Senior citizens: Free cheque book
- Salary package accounts: Free cheque book

Account closure charges (Savings Bank):
- Within 14 days: Free
- After 14 days up to 1 year: Rs.500+GST
- After 1 year: Free

ATM transactions (from Feb 2025):
- SBI ATM: First 5 cash transactions free per month
- Other bank ATMs: First 3 free per month (all centres)
- Beyond free limit: Rs.21+GST per financial transaction
- Non-financial transaction beyond limit: Rs.10+GST

Cash deposits (Savings Bank):
- First 3 transactions per month: Free
- Beyond 3 transactions: Rs.50+GST per transaction

NEFT charges through net banking or mobile: FREE
RTGS charges through net banking or mobile: FREE

NEFT at branch:
- Up to Rs.10,000: Rs.2+GST
- Rs.10,000 to Rs.1 lakh: Rs.4+GST
- Rs.1 lakh to Rs.2 lakh: Rs.12+GST
- Above Rs.2 lakh: Rs.20+GST

RTGS at branch:
- Rs.2 lakh to Rs.5 lakh: Rs.20+GST
- Above Rs.5 lakh: Rs.40+GST

Demand Draft charges:
- Up to Rs.5,000: Rs.25 incl.GST
- Rs.5,000 to Rs.10,000: Rs.50 incl.GST
- Rs.10,000 to Rs.1 lakh: Rs.5 per Rs.1000 min Rs.60
- Above Rs.1 lakh: Rs.4 per Rs.1000 min Rs.600 max Rs.2000

Safe deposit locker annual rent:
- Small (Metro/Urban): Rs.2,000+GST
- Medium (Metro/Urban): Rs.4,000+GST
- Large (Metro/Urban): Rs.8,000+GST
- Extra Large (Metro/Urban): Rs.12,000+GST
- Small (Semi-urban/Rural): Rs.1,500+GST
- Medium (Semi-urban/Rural): Rs.3,000+GST
- Large (Semi-urban/Rural): Rs.6,000+GST
- Extra Large (Semi-urban/Rural): Rs.9,000+GST
- 12 locker visits free per year. Beyond: Rs.100+GST

Debit card issuance:
- Classic/Silver/Global/Contactless: Free
- Gold Debit Card: Rs.100+GST
- Platinum Debit Card: Rs.300+GST

Debit card annual maintenance (from year 2):
- Classic/Silver/Global: Rs.200+GST
- Gold: Rs.250+GST
- Platinum: Rs.325+GST

Stop payment instruction: Rs.100+GST per instrument
max Rs.500+GST per instance
